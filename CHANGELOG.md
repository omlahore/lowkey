# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- `lowkey` skill with four levels: auto (mirrors the room), lite, full and brainrot.
- Hooks that load the rules at session start, keep the level across resume, `/clear` and compaction, and remind the model every turn.
- `lowkey-voice` skill that learns a profile from your own messages.
- Rule copies for Codex, Cursor, Windsurf, Cline, Copilot, Kiro and Gemini CLI, generated from one source.
- Research write-up and a reproducible style benchmark against public GitHub and Hacker News comments.
